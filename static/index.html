How Kavach works

  1. Put the call on speakerKeep the phone close to this laptop.
  2. Pick the language and press Start listeningHindi, Hinglish or Kannada.
  3. Kavach listens and scores the callIf it sounds like a scam, it warns you out loud and shows what to ask the caller.

Scams it knows

    Kavach never hangs up for you, and it can't prove a caller is genuine. Audio is not stored.
